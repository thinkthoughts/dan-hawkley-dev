# About the Role

As an AI Solutions Architect at Tribe AI, you'll sit at the intersection of frontier AI capability and enterprise reality.

You'll serve as the technical bridge between customers and internal AI experts, partnering closely with General Managers (Enterprise AEs) to understand customer needs, define high-impact AI use cases, and architect tailored solutions that can actually ship.

This is not a demo-only role.

You will:

- lead technical discovery
- make architectural tradeoffs
- build clarity in ambiguous environments
- pressure-test ideas through rapid prototypes
- collaborate with frontier model labs including OpenAI and Anthropic
- validate approaches
- stay close to emerging AI capabilities
